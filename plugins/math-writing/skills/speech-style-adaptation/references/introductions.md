# Introductions and thesis structure

Use this reference when drafting or substantially restructuring an
introduction. For a local voice pass, preserve the existing architecture.

## Article introduction

The calibration paper moves through:

1. context (for example, a relevant classical fact), limitation, and
   contribution;
2. setup and the closest prior result;
3. main results, with a construction sketch when useful;
4. a section map and, when customary, acknowledgments.

Use only the stages that serve the paper. Never invent novelty, literature
claims, or motivation to fill the pattern.

## Thesis hierarchy

Keep broad background in the thesis introduction. Part introductions should
frame their common theme; chapter introductions should give local motivation
and results without repeating the global background. Discover the actual
document hierarchy instead of assuming filenames or directories.

For a chapter derived from joint work, retain the mathematical first-person
plural unless the author asks otherwise. Add or change a contribution statement
only from author-supplied facts. Do not infer individual contributions from the
paper or its author list.
